# Problem brief

## Human
Who experiences this?

## Pain
What happens, how often, and how severe is it?

## Current workaround
What do they do today, and why is it inadequate?

## Evidence
Facts, observations, data, and sources.

## Assumptions and unknowns
Rank by impact × uncertainty.

## Why now
Why is this worth attention now?

## Existing-solutions preflight
What maintained tools, libraries, plugins, or free platforms already address it?

## Recommendation
Build, adapt, investigate, buy with approval, or stop.

## Success and stop conditions
Predeclare observable thresholds.
